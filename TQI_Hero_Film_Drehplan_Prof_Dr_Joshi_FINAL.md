# THE QUANTUM IMPERATIVE
## Presenter-Led Hero Film — Final Shooting Plan

**Target runtime:** 88–90 seconds. The final cut may run to approximately 92 seconds if required by Prof. Dr. Joshi’s strongest natural delivery and the final book hold.

## Purpose

A strategic author film for *The Quantum Imperative*. Prof. Dr. Sameer Joshi is the visible narrator. The visual system supports his argument with short, clear transformations; it never replaces him.

> **Visual arc:** Civilization under pressure → dependency becomes visible → leaders regain agency → the future remains open to those who retain the capacity to decide.

## Final script

> The next dependency will not be territorial. It will determine who remains sovereign.
>
> Quantum technologies are no longer arriving alone. They are converging with artificial intelligence, robotics, energy, space, and the systems that shape national and corporate power.
>
> As this stack hardens, the decisive question is no longer which technology to adopt. It is what you still control.
>
> I am Professor Sameer Joshi. Together with my co-author, Thorsten Buehrmann, I wrote *The Quantum Imperative* as an execution architecture for leaders: what to own, what to secure, what to govern, and what to build before dependency becomes sovereignty debt.
>
> The book introduces three practical instruments: the Quantum Covenant Architecture, the Sovereign Stack, and a 100-Day CEO Action Plan.
>
> The future will not belong merely to those with the most advanced technology. It will belong to those who retain the capacity to decide.

**Performance:** Calm, direct and consequential. No sales tone. Pause after “territorial,” “still control,” “sovereignty debt,” and “capacity to decide.”

## Recording brief

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| Camera | 4K UHD, 25 fps, eye level, chest-up medium shot; fixed exposure, focus and white balance. |
| Framing | One centred take for vertical crops; one take with Prof. Dr. Joshi in the left third for graphics. |
| Green screen | Even lighting; subject 1.5–2 m from screen; record a 10-second clean plate. |
| Wardrobe | Solid charcoal, navy or muted deep blue; no green, cyan, patterns, glossy fabric or visible marks. |
| Audio | Lavaliere plus boom backup; 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV; 30 seconds room tone. |
| Takes | Three full takes plus one slower safety take. Direct eye contact for the opening sentence, author introduction and final sentence. |

## Final production rules

Use the established dark blue / cyan / restrained gold visual world. All typography, website details, author names, book-cover artwork and Amazon asset must be added in post-production. Use the approved exact book cover on a realistic 3D book model. Do not use AI-generated lettering or approximate logos.

The final film must show every required phrase fully and clearly: **FIVE DOMAINS. ONE POWER.**; **OWN · SECURE · GOVERN · BUILD**; and **RETAIN THE CAPACITY TO DECIDE.**
