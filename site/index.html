01Materials & Compute

Data, connectivity, and physical inputs

Control critical materials, fabrication pathways, data access, and resilient connectivity instead of mistaking vendor access for capability.

Map single-source exposure from materials to data routes and build substitutable pathways.
02Compute Infrastructure & Quantum Algorithms

From access to auditable capability

Build resilient access to classical and quantum compute, post-quantum cryptography, and deployable algorithmic capability.

Require portability, cryptographic assurance, and testable continuity across compute providers.
03Causal Models & Applications

Own the decision logic

Retain control of high-consequence causal models and application logic rather than renting opaque outcomes from external intelligence.

Make decision-critical models explainable, transferable, and exercisable under stress.
04Governance & Sovereign Auditability

Glass-box computational trust

Make every critical decision traceable, testable, and subject to named human authority with the competence to override it.

Attach evidence, exceptions, counterfactual tests, and accountable sign-off to consequential decisions.
05Talent Sovereignty

The expertise that cannot be rented

Sustain the complete chain of expertise—from devices and cryogenics to software, assurance, operators, and leaders.

Build enduring teams that can operate, challenge, and restore the stack without external permission.
